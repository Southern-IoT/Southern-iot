About Southern IoT

Communication for all, built by people

Behind every connection is a team dedicated to accessible, reliable industrial communication.

Our Story

Democratizing industrial IoT since 2018

Southern IoT was founded in 2018 by engineers who believed industrial connectivity should be precise, secure, and practical enough for the field teams who depend on it every day.

Our motto is communication for all: practical, resilient connectivity that helps teams, communities, and industries stay linked without unnecessary complexity.

Today, Southern IoT supports operators across manufacturing, agriculture, energy, logistics, and urban infrastructure with a platform designed for demanding environments.
